South America Add-On — On the Way Home to Tunis

Great news: As a Tunisian national with a valid US visa, you can enter Colombia and Peru visa-free — no extra paperwork needed. These two countries are hands-down the best value in South America and are both easily reachable by cheap flights from San Francisco. From there, you can fly back to Tunis via a European hub (Madrid, Paris, Rome, Amsterdam). Bogotá → Tunis starts at around $600–700 one-way with one stop.

🇨🇴
Colombia
Bogotá · Medellín · Cartagena · Coffee Region
Colombia has transformed into one of South America's top destinations — warm people, incredible food, colonial cities, Caribbean coast, and coffee mountains. Bogotá is a world-class city with great nightlife, Gold Museum, and Monserrate mountain. Medellín has the famous cable cars and streetart. Cartagena has the stunning walled old town on the Caribbean.

Peru
Lima · Cusco · Machu Picchu · Amazon
Peru is one of the world's great travel destinations — Machu Picchu alone is worth the trip. Lima has a stunning food scene (best in South America), Cusco has Inca ruins everywhere, and the Sacred Valley is breathtaking. A bit pricier than Colombia due to popular tourist routes, but still incredible value compared to the US.

Which one to pick? If you want a shorter, cheaper add-on (5–7 days) → go Colombia. Bogotá is lively, flights are frequent from SFO, and the return to Tunis is easier and cheaper via Madrid. If you want the once-in-a-lifetime bucket list experience → go Peru and do Machu Picchu. It costs a bit more and requires more days to do justice, but Machu Picchu genuinely delivers. You can also do both since Lima to Bogotá is a short 3-hour flight (~$100–200).
